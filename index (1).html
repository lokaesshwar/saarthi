Three recommendations, each tied to a specific number from the dashboard above, with the upside worked out and the assumption it depends on made explicit.

RECOMMENDATION 1

Fix the paywall → payment-initiation drop before touching acquisition

Number & source

funnel query, BigQuery: paywall_shown 18,706 → payment_initiated 5,693 — a 69.6% drop, the steepest in the funnel.

Upside if it works

+5pp conversion (30.4%→35.4%) → 18,706 × 5% ≈ 935 extra initiators. At the current initiation→success rate (4,044/5,693 = 71.0%) → ≈ 664 extra successful payments. At avg net revenue/txn (₹1,217,584 ÷ 3,873 ≈ ₹314) → ≈ ₹208,500 additional net revenue over a comparable 6-month window.

Required assumption

The 5pp improvement is achievable through pricing/paywall-copy changes — this isn't derived from the data, it's a working assumption to size the opportunity. Also assumes the initiation→success rate holds at the margin rather than degrading for marginal converters.

RECOMMENDATION 2

Treat retention as a product problem, not an acquisition problem

Number & source

retention query, BigQuery: 2.0%, 2.0%, 2.1%, 1.7%, 1.9%, 1.7% across six monthly cohorts — flat even as cohort size grew from 4,744 to 11,982. Channel split (web 2.0% vs whatsapp 1.7%) shows it's not acquisition-channel driven.

Upside if it works

In users, not ₹: Aug cohort (11,982 signups) at +1.3pp retention (1.7%→3.0%) → 11,982 × 1.3% ≈ 156 extra returning users/month. Converting this to ₹ needs a query joining transactions to returning sessions — not run yet, flagged as a follow-up.

Required assumption

Assumes the retention ceiling is fixable at the product layer (onboarding, follow-up nudges, re-engagement) rather than being inherent to how the audience uses the product — not something the data alone can confirm.

RECOMMENDATION 3

Shift acquisition effort away from the lowest revenue-per-signup campaign

Number & source

channels query, BigQuery: ta_generic_astro_wide — 10,497 signups, ₹86,134 revenue → ₹8.20/signup, vs ta_career_broad_wa — 11,847 signups, ₹390,193 → ₹32.93/signup. A ~4x gap in revenue efficiency.

Upside if it works

Reallocating effort equivalent to 2,000 signups: 2,000 × (₹32.93 − ₹8.20) = 2,000 × ₹24.73 ≈ ₹49,460 incremental revenue.

Required assumption

Assumes acquisition spend/effort is fungible 1:1 between campaigns and that ta_career_broad_wa's per-signup revenue holds at higher volume (no diminishing returns). No ad-spend/CAC data from meta_ads_daily was queried for this — this is revenue efficiency, not ROI.

What I would deliberately not do

  • Not silently reclassify the channel = 'app' rows (14,093 of them) into "web" or "unknown." No source table confirms what they should be, and guessing would quietly corrupt every channel-attribution number used above.
  • Not recommend a blanket increase in acquisition spend while retention sits flat at ~2%. Pushing more users into a funnel that doesn't retain compounds the leak rather than the revenue — Recommendation 1 and 2 come before spending more to fill the top.
